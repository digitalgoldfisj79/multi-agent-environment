# Method ledger

This ledger prevents equivalent reformulations from being counted as new approaches.

| Lane | Admitted object | Required new information | Promotion test | Kill test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FM` | selected-centre first moment for `Z_j` or a weighted source | cancellation on the actual increasing primorial centres | reaches the I1 necessary scale with all uniformities verified | only dense-centre averaging, conjectural singular-series substitution, or density-one output |
| `4P` | aggregate centred four-prime covariance | cancellation across `j,m,d` with all four prime conditions coupled | gives the residual upper bound with `L=o(log X)` or reduces it to named estimates | termwise four-tuple conjectures, raw absolute values, or a stronger uniform-in-`d` target without a route |
| `2VM` | centred double-von-Mangoldt dispersion | a decomposition preserving the principal term and both prime variables | exact source-to-variance theorem within the loss budget | old reciprocal frame reappears without a proved bridge; norm losses exceed budget |
| `PSD` | deterministic Gram/operator/source-orbit identity | a positive-semidefinite or dual structure that isolates the signed residual | strictly smaller operator theorem with exact implication | operator lower bound already exceeds target scale or baseline mode is lost |
| `FALSIFY` | local-density and primorial-geometry surrogate models | proof of which assumptions are insufficient | explicit countermodel/no-go or surviving discriminator | purely numerical resemblance with no theorem consequence |

## Excluded by default

- Paper VII cubic geometry;
- direct function-field `d=1`;
- more pair-sum moments;
- random-order concentration;
- generic short-interval prime averages;
- finite scans used to claim uniform asymptotics.

An excluded method can re-enter only with a committed theorem mapping it to the frozen centred detector and a complete exponent audit.
