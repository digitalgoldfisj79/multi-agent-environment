## Compliance definition

A paper is **Lean compliant** when every load-bearing theorem is classified as one of:

- `KERNEL_CHECKED`;
- `DERIVED_WITH_LEDGERED_AXIOM`;
- `EXACT_COMPUTATIONAL`;
- `MANUSCRIPT_PROVED_NOT_YET_FORMALIZED`;
- `OPEN_OR_CONDITIONAL`.

The manuscript, claim ledger and formal package must agree on that classification. Lean compliance does not imply full formalization of the paper.
