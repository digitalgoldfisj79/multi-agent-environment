# Reproducibility boundary — conditional/barriers synthesis

The synthesis is mathematically readable without executing code. Its exact finite Bonferroni implication and deterministic obstruction lemmas are presented in the manuscript. Supporting code checks the beta=5 parameter arithmetic, exact local connected coefficients, finite squarefree-collision ledgers, and Lean counterparts of selected deterministic implications.

The beta=5 certificate assumes `epsilon=0.10` and `U_b/L_b<=1.10`. Finite computations do not establish the jointly signed selected-centre prime-tuple hypothesis, any Hardy--Littlewood asymptotic at primorial centres, or Fortune's conjecture. Those remain explicit analytic premises/open problems.
