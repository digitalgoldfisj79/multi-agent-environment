## Abstract

For primes \(p\equiv5\pmod6\), a cubic trace problem arising from a function-field Fortune conjecture reduces to the difference of Frobenius traces on the \(\mu_3\)-invariant parts of two adjacent symmetric powers of the rank-two cubic Airy sheaf. Both spaces have rank \((p-5)/6\), and the required estimate is an absolute correlation bound at the exceptional boundary where the symmetric-power index equals the characteristic.

The paper proves exact collapse identities, zero Swan conductor for the \(p\)-th Adams virtual sheaf, an odd-power trace-extraction lemma, and an unconditional \(O(\sqrt p)\)-coefficient bound. It records exact low-rank spectra through rank four and independently reproduced third-power traces. The absolute correlation theorem and the object-level Airy-to-Fortune application bridge remain open.
