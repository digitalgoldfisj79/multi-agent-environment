**Date:** 4 August 2026

## 1. Integer Fortune lane

The only currently valid load-bearing chain is

```text
Fortune for every centre
    ⇐ corrected all-centres detector criterion
    ⇐ prime-pair block variance with loss o(log X)
    ⇐ deterministic signed source/orbit or normalized-survivor transfer theorem
```

Paper II proves candidate collapse, the corrected detector identities and the exact variance implications. Paper III supplies kernel rigidity and an exact four-prime covariance formulation. Neither paper proves the final variance estimate.

Paper I supplies structural collision and rank theorems, but explicitly leaves both its internal centred energy estimates and every signed prime-detection bridge open. Paper IV proves the reciprocal estimate only after averaging over random prime orderings. It does not cover the unique increasing primorial ordering and does not repair the corrected detector bridge.

Therefore the integer frontier is one theorem class:

`INT-SIGNED-TRANSFER` — retain the corrected prime-pair source, principal subtraction and physical cancellation through a deterministic transfer strong enough to give the block variance.

Old HTE4 or random-order derandomisation work is secondary unless it is reconnected to this corrected source by a proved theorem.

## 2. Direct function-field d=1 lane

```text
function-field d=1 crown
    ⇔ W_p > 0 for every admitted prime
    ⇐ N_sq + N_ns > 0 for at least one cubic class
    ⇔ strict invariant q-line nonsaturation
```

Paper V proves the exact normal forms, orbit ledger, crown variable and q-line equivalence. Paper VI constructs Cartier, Hattori–Stallings, Artin–Schreier and Kummer carriers, but every route returns to the same one-sided positivity problem.

Therefore the direct d=1 frontier is:

`D1-QLINE-NONSAT` — exclude simultaneous q-line saturation/failure by a genuinely one-sided Frobenius or rational-point theorem.

There is no theorem transferring this crown to integer Fortune.

## 3. Paper VII finite-field endpoint lane

The stable chain is

```text
bilateral endpoint incidence
    ⇒ inverse-free four-divisibility model
    ⇒ unique common defect
    ⇒ zero-defect translation/reflection or nonzero-defect locus
```

Zero defect is classified and the strip is empty. The quadratic nonzero-defect locus is certificate-empty over every odd prime power. Cubic true incidences exist, so universal emptiness is false.

The sole existence-side frontier is:

`P7-CUBIC-TF` — a theorem about true Frobenius-oriented points on the faithful saturated cubic locus.

This is not the direct d=1 q-line theorem and has no proved integer interface.
