# House style: aesthetic / satisfying

Every post looks like it came from the same account. Trends change the subject, not the look.

## Fixed visual signature (append to every prompt)
`Warm amber and cream colour palette, soft golden window light, shallow depth of field, 35mm film grain, calm, cozy, deeply satisfying. No people, no faces, no text, no logos, no brand marks.`

Seasonal swaps are allowed only for the palette's accent colour (for example cool blue-hour windows in winter or green in spring). Keep amber/cream as the base.

## Reel (1 per day): Kling 3.0 Turbo, 10 s, 9:16, 720p, 15 credits
- One continuous satisfying action: a pour, a slice, a melt, a fold, a bloom, steam or snow. Static, locked-off camera, macro or close.
- The hook has to land in the first second, so the action is already in motion at frame 1.
- Keep the clip's native audio when it has some. If it's silent, run `engine/ambient_audio.py`, which makes an original synthesised pad.
- Template: `Extreme macro slow-motion shot: <trend subject doing one satisfying action>. <2-3 texture details>. <light + background>. <SIGNATURE>. Smooth continuous motion, static locked-off camera.`

## Carousel (1 per day): GPT Image 2.6, 5 images, 4:5, quality medium, jpeg, 0.5 credits each
1. Hook: the hero overhead or flat-lay of the trend subject.
2. Macro texture.
3. Place or mood (window, room, landscape).
4. Process or detail.
5. Close: a quieter end-of-day version of the scene.

## Captions
- One lowercase line under about 80 characters, then a soft call to action ("save it for…", "which one would you step into?"). No engagement bait like "comment YES".
- 5 hashtags maximum: 1-2 from the trend, plus 3 from the evergreen pool `#cozyaesthetic #satisfying #slowliving #cafevibes #aesthetic`.
- Always end with `(AI-generated visuals)`.
