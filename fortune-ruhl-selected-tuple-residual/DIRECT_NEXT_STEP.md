# Direct-path ruling

There is no justified successor build inside the present mechanism family.

A future programme is admissible only if it begins with a genuinely new theorem statement that estimates the jointly signed selected-centre prime-tuple residual through order `Theta(log X)` and immediately implies the frozen RUHL margin. It must not begin with another divisor identity, generic modulus class, local cluster majorant, or finite census.

Until such an input is identified, the correct action is consolidation rather than another autonomous programme.
