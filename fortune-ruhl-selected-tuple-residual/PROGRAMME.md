# FORTUNE_RUHL_SELECTED_TUPLE_RESIDUAL_V0_1

## Scope

This programme is allowed to work only on the already-proved direct chain

\[
\mathrm{RUHL\!\!-\!FM}\Longrightarrow\mathrm{INT\!\!-\!AOD}
\Longrightarrow\text{eventual Fortune}.
\]

It may not open function-field, Paper VII, random-order, new detector, generic PWOC, or local-cluster side lanes.

## Frozen parameters

For each deterministic terminal-prime stratum put

\[
M_b=n_bB,
\qquad
q_b=(1+3\varepsilon)\frac{\log M_b}{L_b},
\qquad
K_b=\lceil5\log M_b\rceil_{\rm even},
\]

with `epsilon=0.10`, `U_b/L_b<=1.10`, `L_b asymp X`, and the inherited geometry `M_b asymp X/log X`.

The sharp absolute residual margin is

\[
\Delta_b=\frac1{M_b}-e^{-q_bL_b}
-\frac{(q_bU_b)^{K_b+1}}{(K_b+1)!}.
\]

## Gates

### R0 — direct-chain freeze

Freeze the RUHL-FM implication and prohibit parallel frontier work.

### R1 — exact signed residual

Write

\[
\mathcal E_{b,K_b}
=\sum_{k=0}^{K_b}\frac{(-q_b)^k}{k!}E_{b,k}
\]

as the difference between the averaged even Bonferroni polynomial of the actual occupancies and the averaged Taylor polynomial of the deterministic model means.

### R2 — absolute first-order embedding

Determine the exact consequence of

\[
\sum_{k\le K_b}\frac{q_b^k}{k!}|E_{b,k}|<\Delta_b
\]

for `E_{b,1}`. No higher-order estimate may conceal this gate.

### R3 — model and correction ledger

Keep the indicator formulation correction-free. If a von Mangoldt source is introduced, quantify the output-prime-power correction against the first-order allowance.

### R4 — one source identity

\[

\quad(n\le z^J).
