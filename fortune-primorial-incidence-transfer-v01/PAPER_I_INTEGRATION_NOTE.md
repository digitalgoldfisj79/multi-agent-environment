`|A_X(varpi)| >> (X/(log X)^2)^(8/15)`.

Thus the logical distinction is:

- old Paper I: much stronger almost-injectivity for typical/averaged moduli at the large-divisor scale;
- new theorem: weaker support exponent but pointwise in the modulus, including the small comparable-modulus regime `varpi ~ X`.
