## 1. Load-bearing checks

### Frame expansion

The expansion of the frame energy into the deterministic equal-modulus term and the distinct-modulus term is algebraically correct. The phase multiplier is

`b = a(r - q)`

modulo `qr`, and the stated size and coprimality conditions hold because `q,r` are of size `H`, while the block primes and `a` are smaller.

### Configuration enumeration

The coefficient patterns for differences of two pair-sums are complete:

- two support indices: `(1,-1)` and `(2,-2)`, with signs reversed;
- three support indices: one coefficient of magnitude two and two of magnitude one;
- four support indices: two `+1` and two `-1`.

The sliding family has multiplicity `N`; all other net coefficient configurations have multiplicity one. The completeness identity is consistent.

### Ordered-partition identity and contour bound

Conditioning on ranks produces a uniform ordered set partition of the block primes. The coefficient-extraction formula is exact. The Cauchy-contour estimate and the pairwise ratio-character deficit identity are valid, including deletion of empty cells and the `K^2` Stirling prefactor.

### Gauss and CRT coefficients

The multiplicative-character expansion on units modulo `qr` has the required norms:

- coefficient sup norm of order `1/sqrt(qr)`;
- coefficient l2 norm exactly one;
- coefficient l1 norm at most `sqrt(qr)`.

The two-slot grouped coefficient has sup norm at most one by Cauchy-Schwarz. Principal components are smaller and do not create a distinct-modulus main term.

### Bad-character count

The sixth-moment argument is correct. Products of three block primes are strictly below `qr`, so a congruence between two such products is integer equality. Unique factorization gives at most six right triples per left triple. Chebyshev at correlation threshold `3/4` yields

`beta <= C eta^2 X (log X)^3`.

This is the sole arithmetic input and is exactly strong enough for the binding ledger class.

### Matching lemma and ledger

The rooted-path coordinate change is triangular. Summing coordinates from the outside toward the macro cell consumes each edge once. Good coordinates pay the l1 group mass and receive contour decay; bad coordinates pay `beta` times the group sup norm. The all-bad pattern dominates after the selected `C_3 = 600` decay.

The binding one-micro-cell classes have raw scale

`X^2 (log X)^7 = M (log X)^9`,

so the claimed exponent nine is correct. Replacing the bad-character count by `X^(4/3)` would fail, confirming that the margin is only polylogarithmic.

### Aggregate assembly

The diagonal term is controlled by the frame lower bound. The distinct-modulus sum uses `sum_a m_a = 1/2`. Schwartz decay controls `a >= H`. The Frobenius-energy step follows from the Paper-II algebraic inequality and the same diagonal and distinct-modulus estimates.

## 2. Scope

The theorem is a random-order model theorem. Its decay is generated by order entropy. No part of the argument transfers to the increasing primorial order without an additional deterministic comparison or transfer theorem.
