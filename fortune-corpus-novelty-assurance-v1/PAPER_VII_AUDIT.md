## P7.6 Genuine normalization

The manuscript and Round-15 source provide an explicit degree-two normalization:

- translate the first quadratic to `t^2+A`;
- normalize the homothety parameter;
- write the second quadratic as `t^2+Bt+C` and the defect parameter as `U`;
- reduce `t^q-t` modulo an irreducible quadratic using its conjugate root;
- expand the four endpoint divisibilities to obtain exactly `f0,f1,f2,f3`;
- verify the arithmetic-open conditions and chart cover.

This is mathematically explicit, but it is not currently represented in the Lean `Datum` layer. Formalising it requires a materially larger finite-field polynomial algebraisation: degree-two polynomial normal forms, remainders of `X^q-X`, divisibility transport, and coefficient extraction.
