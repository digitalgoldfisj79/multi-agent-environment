## Non-negotiable distinctions

1. An identity is not an estimate.
2. A local singular-series average is not a prime-tuple count.
3. A conditional Hardy-Littlewood benchmark is not an unconditional Fortune result.
4. A density-one result does not resolve one failed row.
5. A weighting chosen after seeing successful output primes is circular and inadmissible.
6. A finite-panel success is not an asymptotic theorem.
