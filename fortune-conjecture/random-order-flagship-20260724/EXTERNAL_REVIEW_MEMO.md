# External review memorandum

## Result under review

For a uniformly random ordering of the primes in `[X,2X)`, form the nested prefix products and all unordered pair sums of the resulting `N` centres. Sample pair-sum differences through a reciprocal Fourier frame with prime moduli `q,r ~ X^2`. The manuscript proves

`E_sigma E_a <= C M (log X)^9`

uniformly for all natural harmonics `1 <= |a| < eta X^2`, together with the weighted aggregate and Frobenius-energy versions.

## Why it may be interesting

- It proves the critical reciprocal-energy target in a model with the same primes, pair lift, moduli, and harmonic range as the deterministic primorial problem.
- It requires no GRH or pointwise character-sum estimate.
- Random order is exploited exactly through ordered set partitions, not approximated by independent cells.
- A sixth-moment count of exceptional characters is precisely strong enough to close the binding configuration.

## What it does not prove

The increasing order is one deterministic ordering and receives no permutation entropy. The theorem does not imply Fortune's conjecture or a prime after every primorial.

## Questions for the reviewer

1. Is the main theorem new in substance, or is it subsumed by a known theorem on random permutations, multiplicative walks, or character sums?
2. Is the contour/matching/ledger proof complete and uniform in all parameters?
3. Is the result independently interesting without the Fortune motivation?
4. Which journal level and mathematical framing would be appropriate?

## High-risk proof locations

- Ratio-character contour decay.
- Triangular coordinate bijection and orphan slots.
- C2a/C2b/C2d binding ledger.
- Passage from the fixed-harmonic estimate to the weighted aggregate.
