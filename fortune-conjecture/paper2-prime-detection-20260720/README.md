## Scope

The paper proves exact reductions, moment formulas, and route-specific obstruction theorems. It isolates two open theorem boundaries:

1. a complete shifted-von-Mangoldt block variance at primorial centres;
2. a weighted one-sided reciprocal sampling estimate for the consecutive-prime prefix-product walk.

Neither open estimate is proved.
