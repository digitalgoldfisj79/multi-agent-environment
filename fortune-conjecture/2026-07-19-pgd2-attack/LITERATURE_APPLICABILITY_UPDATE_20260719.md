# Literature applicability update — 2026-07-19

The new surviving target is a globally coupled, growing-Möbius-degree reciprocal operator. The following recent primary sources were checked.

## Pascadi, arXiv:2511.08445

`Non-abelian amplification and bilinear forms with Kloosterman sums` proves power-saving bounds for bilinear forms in complete Kloosterman sums at a fixed composite modulus, including balanced square-root-length variables.

The PGD2 detector has no complete inner unit variable and its conductor changes with the divisor migration `n=dm`. Producing a complete Kloosterman sum requires separating variables and conductors before the cancellation across Möbius degrees, which is exactly the prohibited conditioning loss. No direct application was found.

## Milićević–Qin–Wu, arXiv:2511.07550

`Bilinear forms with Kloosterman sums and moments of twisted L-functions` also treats general bilinear forms in complete Kloosterman sums modulo a fixed admissible modulus. The same mismatch applies: the Fortune operator is a signed average over varying denominator factors, not a fixed-conductor complete Kloosterman form.

## Zheng, arXiv:2512.22798

`Primes in simultaneous arithmetic progressions` proves mean-value estimates for primes satisfying two linear congruences, with divisor-bounded and well-factorable coefficient sequences. The PGD2 phase is an exponential reciprocal kernel with primorial-dependent residues and no fixed pair of linear congruence classes. The theorem does not absorb the growing-degree detector or its required cross-degree cancellation.

## Granville–Lamzouri, arXiv:2604.02306

`Large values of exponential sums with multiplicative coefficients` studies sums of the form `sum f(n)e(n alpha)`, including smooth-number restrictions and pretentious obstructions. PGD2 instead has reciprocal denominator phase `e(L/n)`, with `L` exponentially large and averaged over a structured pair-sum family. The results do not supply the required reciprocal-modulus mean square.

## Verdict

No checked theorem controls the cumulative degree-truncated Möbius detector while preserving:

- all Möbius-degree cancellation;
- varying reciprocal conductors;
- the primorial pair-sum average;
- exact Frobenius centring.

The literature boundary remains a new signed variable-conductor dispersion theorem rather than a missing black-box application.
