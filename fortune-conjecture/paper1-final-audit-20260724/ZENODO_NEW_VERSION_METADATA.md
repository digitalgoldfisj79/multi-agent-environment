**Description**

This version supplies the named circulation manuscript and a hardened publication package for *Collision Geometry and Spectral Laws for Consecutive-Prime Partial Products*. The mathematical body is unchanged from the anonymously reviewed manuscript contained in version 1. The changes are author/ORCID metadata, explicit data and code availability, AI-assistance disclosure, deterministic PDF/DOCX production, exact source and binary checksums, a live-Zenodo fidelity audit, fresh exact-hash hostile review, and page-by-page compiled-artifact quality assurance.

The paper proves structural results on multiplicative collisions of consecutive-prime prefix products, including exact character moments, low-transport and offset-slice incidence bounds, average almost-injectivity, an interval endpoint-graph Smith-form theorem, pair-overlap and median decompositions, independent-prefix spectral laws, support-family and block-composition bounds, additive-frequency estimates and common-translation obstructions.

The centered estimates HTE4, HWF4, FBHE4 and RQHE4 remain open. The paper does not provide a prime-detection bridge, a prime-offset theorem or a proof of Fortune's conjecture.
