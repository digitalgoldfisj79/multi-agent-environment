# Primary-source literature ledger

## Factorial walks

1. Garaev–Luca–Shparlinski, *Character Sums and Congruences with n!*, Trans. AMS 356 (2004), 5089–5102. arXiv:math/0403422. Load-bearing mechanism: shift averaging and Weil bounds for rational functions built from \(\prod_{i\le k}(n+i)\). Individual bound \(N^{3/4}p^{1/8}(\log p)^{1/4}\).
2. Garaev–Luca–Shparlinski, *Exponential Sums and Congruences with Factorials*, J. reine angew. Math. 584 (2005), 29–44. arXiv:math/0403424. Load-bearing mechanism: additive orthogonality and root counts for bounded-degree shifted-product polynomials.
3. Klurman–Munsch, *Polynomial Products Modulo Primes and Applications*, Monatsh. Math. 191 (2020), 577–593. arXiv:1810.06310. Generalises factorials only to products of values of a fixed polynomial; algebraicity remains essential.
4. Grebennikov–Sagdeev–Semchankau–Vasilevskii, *On the sequence n! mod p*, Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 40 (2024), 637–648, DOI 10.4171/RMI/1422. Uses image/intersection estimates for \(P_j(x)=(x+1)\cdots(x+j)\) and Lang–Weil-type algebraic geometry.
5. Garaev–Pardo, *Additive congruences with factorials modulo a prime*, arXiv:2508.12127 (2025). Its core input remains the 2005 diagonal factorial congruence theorem and the 2024 factorial image-set work.

## Structured random matrices

1. Alt–Erdős–Krüger, *Local law for random Gram matrices*, Electron. J. Probab. 22 (2017), arXiv:1606.07353. Assumes independent centred entries.
2. Fleermann–Heiny, *Large Sample Covariance Matrices of Gaussian Observations with Uniform Correlation Decay*, arXiv:2203.04057. Assumes a correlated joint Gaussian model with uniform correlation decay.
3. Fan–Ma–Paquette–Wang, *Anisotropic local law for non-separable sample covariance matrices*, arXiv:2602.17960 (2026). Assumes independent random sample vectors and concentration/cumulant conditions.

None is a black-box theorem for the deterministic primorial walk matrix.
