# Explicit Artin--Schreier coordinate for the p-cycle quotient

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Status:** exact cyclic-resolvent theorem proved; global quotient complexity remains open.

## 1. Cyclically ordered roots

Let

`F(X)=X^p+aX^3+cX+d`, `a!=0`,

and work in its generic `S_p` splitting field. Choose a p-cycle

`sigma=(0 1 ... p-1)`

and label the roots so that

`sigma(x_i)=x_(i+1)`.

Let

`s_k=sum_(i in F_p) x_i^k`

be the k-th power sum.

## 2. Newton vanishing

The coefficients of

`X^(p-1),...,X^4,X^2`

are zero. Newton's identities therefore give

`s_k=0` for `1<=k<=p-4`.

At `k=p-3`, the only nonzero coefficient entering Newton's identity is the coefficient a of `X^3`:

`s_(p-3)+(p-3)a=0`.

In characteristic p, `p-3=-3`, hence

`boxed(s_(p-3)=3a.)`

This is the same trace identity that normalizes the Frobenius and Cartier cofactors.

## 3. A translation coordinate

Put

`D=sum_(i in F_p) i x_i^(p-3)`

and

`w=-D/(3a)`.

Reindexing cyclically gives

`sigma(D)=sum_i i x_(i+1)^(p-3)`
`        =sum_j (j-1)x_j^(p-3)`
`        =D-s_(p-3)`
`        =D-3a.`

Therefore:

### Theorem ECR.1

`boxed(sigma(w)=w+1.)`

Consequently

`h=w^p-w`

is fixed by the p-cycle subgroup C and belongs to the quotient field `M=L^C`.

The cyclic degree-p extension `L/M` is exactly

`boxed(w^p-w=h.)`

## 4. Character-sheaf realization

Let psi be a nontrivial character of C. Under the cyclic-induction identity

`Lambda_p=Ind_C^(S_p)(1-psi)`,

the rank-one character psi is the Artin--Schreier character attached to the explicit function h above.

Thus the abstract quotient formulation has become concrete:

- the quotient is the cyclic-ordering field `M=L^C`;
- the p-extension is generated by the weighted power-sum resolvent w;
- the inducing sheaf is the rank-one Artin--Schreier sheaf `L_psi(h)`.

## 5. Degree and ramification

The resolvent w has polynomial degree `p-3` in the cyclically ordered roots. This linear complexity is consistent with the previously proved lower bound `m>=p/4` for any rational Artin--Schreier semiconjugacy witness.

At wild infinity, the cyclic extension has lower jump `(p-3)/2`; after Artin--Schreier reduction, h has a pole of that order on the relevant quotient branch.

## 6. Remaining issue

The quotient `M=L^C` has degree `(p-1)!` over the coefficient field and is not yet explicitly parametrized. The exact resolvent does not by itself bound its genus or total Betti complexity.

The next target is to exploit the critical-value normal form

`qX^p+X^3-3X+(q-2)d`

to express h on a bounded-complexity model of the quotient, or to show directly that the relative cohomology of `1-L_psi(h)` has numerator-plus-denominator degree O(p) on the q-line.
