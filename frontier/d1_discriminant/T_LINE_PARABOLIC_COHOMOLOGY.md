# Fixed-q parabolic cohomology of the p-cycle character

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Status:** exact virtual Euler characteristic and middle-rank calculation proved; effectivity and q-line conductor remain open.

## 1. Normalized cover

For `q notin {0,2,infinity}`, consider

`f_q(z)=(q z^p+z^3-3z)/(q-2)`.

Its derivative is

`f_q'(z)=3(z^2-1)/(q-2)`.

Thus the finite critical points are `+1,-1`, both simple, and their critical values are `+1,-1`.

The root cover over the t-line is therefore lisse on

`U=P^1_t minus {+1,-1,infinity}`.

Its geometric monodromy is `S_p`.

Let

`Lambda_p=sum_(i=0)^(p-1)(-1)^i exterior^i(Perm_p-1)`

be the p-cycle virtual representation, and let `L_q` be the corresponding virtual l-adic local system on U.

## 2. Local data

At `t=+1` and `t=-1`, inertia is generated by a transposition. The restriction of `Lambda_p` to this order-two group is zero in the rational representation ring, because its character is zero both at the identity and at the transposition.

Hence the virtual rank and all finite Swan conductors are zero.

At infinity, `WILD_INFINITY_INERTIA.md` proves

`I_infinity=C_p semidirect C_((p-1)/2)`

with lower jump `(p-3)/2`, and

`Swan_infinity(Lambda_p)=-(p-3)`.

## 3. Grothendieck--Ogg--Shafarevich

The virtual rank of `L_q` is zero. Therefore the rank-times-Euler-characteristic term vanishes, and GOS gives

`chi_c(U_bar,L_q)`
` = -sum_x Swan_x(L_q)`
` = p-3.`

### Theorem TPC.1

`boxed(chi_c(U_bar,L_q)=p-3.)`

This is independent of q on the generic three-point locus.

## 4. Top and middle cohomology

Compactly supported `H^0` vanishes on the affine curve U.

The multiplicity of the trivial representation in `Lambda_p` is one:

`(1/|S_p|)sum_(sigma in S_p)Tr(sigma|Lambda_p)=1`,

because the character equals p on the `(p-1)!` p-cycles and zero elsewhere.

Consequently

`dim H_c^2(U_bar,L_q)=1`

as a virtual dimension, and arithmetic Frobenius acts on this constant line with trace p.

Since

`chi_c=-dim H_c^1+dim H_c^2`,

Theorem TPC.1 gives:

### Theorem TPC.2

`boxed(dim H_c^1(U_bar,L_q)=4-p)`

as a virtual dimension.

The negative value records cancellation in the virtual representation; it is not the dimension of an actual vector space.

## 5. Fibre-count trace formula

Let `n(q)` denote the number of t in `F_p` for which the corresponding degree-p fibre is irreducible. At the two singular finite fibres the p-cycle virtual stalk is zero, so the trace sum over U equals the full affine fibre count.

Grothendieck--Lefschetz gives

`boxed(p n(q)=p-Tr(Frob_q|H_c^1(U_bar,L_q)).)`

The same formula applies to the split and nonsplit universal q-families in `CRITICAL_VALUE_NORMAL_FORM.md`.

Thus the centred fixed-q count

`n(q)-1`

is exactly `-1/p` times the Frobenius trace of a virtual middle-cohomology object of linear virtual rank.

## 6. What this proves and what it does not

It proves that the apparent exterior-power complexity collapses globally on each t-line from `2^(p-1)` to virtual rank `p-4`.

It does not by itself give a Riemann-hypothesis bound, because a virtual class of rank `4-p` may be represented as a difference of actual sheaves with much larger total rank. One must show effectivity, or otherwise control the total degree of the numerator and denominator of the associated Artin L-function.

## 7. q-line target

Let `K_+` and `K_-` be the fixed-q middle-cohomology complexes for the two universal families. The crown follows from an absolute linear-complexity statement:

### Lemma QLC

The semisimplified numerator-plus-denominator ranks and total conductors of `K_+`, `K_-` and their quadratic Kummer twists on the q-line are `O(p)` with an absolute implied constant.

Indeed Deligne's curve bound would then give

`sum_q n_+(q)=p+O(sqrt(p))`,

`sum_q n_-(q)=p+O(sqrt(p))`,

and quadratic-twisted sums `O(sqrt(p))`, after dividing the unnormalized p-cycle traces by p.

Combined with `CRITICAL_VALUE_NORMAL_FORM.md`, this yields

`N_a(p)=p+O(sqrt(p))`

and proves positivity for all sufficiently large p, with the remaining primes covered by the existing finite certification.

## 8. Relevant primary machinery

The use of local wild conductor and characteristic-cycle localisation is consistent with the Abbes--Saito and Takeshi Saito conductor frameworks. Those theories compute Euler characteristics and vanishing-cycle conductors from local ramification data. They do not automatically establish Lemma QLC; numerator/denominator effectivity remains the precise missing point.
