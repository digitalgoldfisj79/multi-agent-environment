# Full root-marking monodromy for compatible cubic factors

**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Status:** the geometric monodromy of the eight compatible oriented cubics and their roots is proved to be `C_3^8 semidirect S_8`.

## 1. Orientation monodromy

`CUBIC_MONODROMY.md` proves that the degree-eight compatible-orientation cover has generic arithmetic and geometric monodromy `S_8`.

Over a field containing `s = sqrt(-3)`, an oriented separable cubic

`h(X)=X^3+uX+v`, `V^2=-4u^3-27v^2`,

has cyclic root field generated by a cube root of the Cardano class

`q = (-9v+sV)/18`.

Indeed, if `A^3=q`, then a root is

`A-u/(3A)`.

Let `q_1,...,q_8` be the eight generic classes over the splitting field of the orientation eliminant. The Galois group of their compositum over that splitting field is an `S_8`-stable subgroup

`K <= F_3^8`.

## 2. The possible S8-stable kernels

Let D be the diagonal line generated by `(1,...,1)` and let

`W = {(x_1,...,x_8): sum x_i=0}`.

### Lemma CRM.1

The only nonzero `S_8`-stable subspaces of `F_3^8` are

`D`, `W`, and `F_3^8`.

### Proof

If a stable subspace contains a vector with two unequal coordinates, subtract its image under the transposition of those coordinates. This produces a nonzero multiple of `e_i-e_j`. Conjugating gives every difference, hence W. If the subspace then contains a vector whose coordinate sum is nonzero, it is the full permutation module. Otherwise it is W. A stable subspace in which every vector has equal coordinates is D. QED.

The single marked-root cover is connected: it is the irreducible trace-zero plane cover constructed in `ORIENTED_CUBIC_PARAMETERIZATION.md`. Thus K is nonzero and is one of these three groups.

## 3. The diagonal kernel is impossible

At the rational coefficient pair

`c=-5`, `d=-4`,

the compatibility equations have the two rational oriented solutions

`(V,u,v) = (13,-13/3,-65/27)`

and

`(V,u,v) = (7,-7,7)`.

The associated cubics are

`h_13(X)=X^3-(13/3)X-65/27`,

`h_7(X)=X^3-7X+7`.

After replacing `Z=3X` in the first field, integral defining polynomials are

`Z^3-39Z-65`,

`Z^3-7Z+7`.

Both are irreducible and Galois. Their number-field discriminants are respectively

`13^2` and `7^2`.

Hence they define distinct cyclic cubic fields. If K were the diagonal subgroup D, all eight generic cyclic root extensions would be the same extension, and every good specialization would identify these two fields. This specialization contradicts that. Therefore

`K != D`.

## 4. The sum-zero kernel is impossible

If `K=W`, Kummer duality says that the diagonal relation is the unique nonzero relation among the eight classes:

`product_(i=1)^8 q_i` is a cube.

We test this at the `S_8` specialization `c=d=-2` from `CUBIC_MONODROMY.md`.

The first subresultant of the compatibility equations is linear in u. Substituting its rational expression for u into

`q=(-9v+sV)/18`

and taking the resultant over all eight roots of the orientation eliminant gives the exact product

`P = 11779(2089+1983s)/(2*3^21)`,

where `s^2=-3`.

### Lemma CRM.2

P is not a cube in `Q(s)`.

### Proof

Suppose

`P=(a+bs)^3`, `a,b in Q`.

The rational part of P is nonzero, so `a != 0`. Put `t=b/a`. Comparing the ratio of the s-coefficient to the rational coefficient gives

`1983/2089 = (3t-3t^3)/(1-9t^2)`.

Therefore t is a rational root of

`2089t^3-5949t^2-2089t+661`.

Modulo 5 this is

`4t^3+t^2+t+1`,

which has no root in `F_5`. The cubic is therefore irreducible over Q and has no rational root. Contradiction. QED.

Thus `K != W`.

## 5. Full marked-root monodromy

The only remaining possibility is

`K=F_3^8`.

### Theorem CRM.3

Over an algebraically closed constant field of characteristic zero, the full geometric monodromy of the eight compatible oriented cubics together with their marked roots is

`C_3^8 semidirect S_8`.

The `S_8` factor permutes the eight cubic blocks and each `C_3` rotates the three roots inside one block independently.

## 6. Fibre-power consequence

For every `1 <= j <= 8`, the group is transitive on data consisting of

- an ordered j-tuple of distinct compatible cubic blocks; and
- one marked root in each selected block.

Consequently every ordered distinct j-factor marked-root fibre power is geometrically irreducible.

More generally, adding root markings to any chosen subset of the j factors remains geometrically irreducible. This is the exact connectedness input required to expand the cubic irreducibility projectors in factorial-moment calculations.

The orders `j>8` remain supported on the fixed exceptional divisor of the compatible map and contribute only lower-dimensional errors.

## 7. Next consequence

Combining this theorem with the local-root cubic cover yields the predicted locally admissible cubic factorial moments

`Q_(a,3,j) = p^2/(j! 3^(j+1)) + O(p^(3/2))`

for `1 <= j <= 8`, provided the finite local-root mixed covers are geometrically integral. The monodromy obstacle is now completely removed; only those fixed Kummer/root-cover audits remain.
