# The mod-p Adams/Frobenius exact sequence at the critical symmetric power

**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Scope:** function-field `d=1` Fortune sibling only.  
**Status:** the rank-two representation and connection theorem is **PROVED**. Its application to a characteristic-zero Frobenius trace bound is **OPEN**.

## 1. Statement

Let `S` be a scheme of characteristic `p>=3` and let `E` be a rank-two vector bundle on `S`. Let `F_S` denote absolute Frobenius. There is a canonical short exact sequence

\[
\boxed{
0\longrightarrow F_S^*E
\stackrel{\iota}{\longrightarrow}
\operatorname{Sym}^p E
\longrightarrow
\det(E)\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^{p-2}E
\longrightarrow0.
}
\]

Consequently, in `K_0(S)`,

\[
\boxed{
[\operatorname{Sym}^pE]
-[\det(E)\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^{p-2}E]
=[F_S^*E].
}
\]

Thus the characteristic-zero virtual Adams expression becomes an actual rank-two Frobenius pullback after reduction to characteristic `p`.

## 2. Proof in a local frame

Choose a local frame `X,Y` of `E`. The injection is

\[
F_S^*E\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}^pE,
\qquad
X^{(p)}\mapsto X^p,
\qquad
Y^{(p)}\mapsto Y^p.
\]

It is well-defined under a frame change because

\[
(aX+bY)^p=a^pX^p+b^pY^p.
\]

Its image is the rank-two subbundle generated by `X^p,Y^p`.

Modulo this subbundle, the mixed monomials

\[
X^{p-i}Y^i,\qquad1\le i\le p-1,
\]

form a basis. Define

\[
X^{p-i}Y^i
\longmapsto
i\,(X\wedge Y)\otimes X^{p-1-i}Y^{i-1}.
\]

Since `i` is nonzero in characteristic `p`, this is an isomorphism of the quotient with `det(E) tensor Sym^(p-2)E` as a vector bundle.

It is `GL_2`-equivariant. For an upper unipotent frame change, equivariance is the binomial identity

\[
\binom{i}{j}j=i\binom{i-1}{j-1}.
\]

For a lower unipotent frame change, it is

\[
\binom{p-i}{h}(i+h)
=i\binom{p-1-i}{h}
\quad\text{in characteristic }p.
\]

Diagonal matrices are immediate from the weights, and diagonal plus upper and lower unipotents generate `GL_2`. This proves exactness and canonicity.

The coefficient identities are checked independently by `mod_p_adams_sequence_verify.py`.

## 3. Compatibility with connections

Suppose `E` carries a connection `nabla`. The induced connection on `Sym^p E` preserves the Frobenius-power subbundle because locally

\[
\nabla(X^p)=pX^{p-1}\nabla X=0,
\qquad
\nabla(Y^p)=0.
\]

Under the identification above, this subconnection is the Cartier connection on `F_S^*E`, and the quotient connection is the connection induced on

\[
\det(E)\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^{p-2}E.
\]

Hence the displayed sequence is exact in the category of vector bundles with connection in characteristic `p`.

## 4. Relevance to the cubic Airy problem

The missing global trace is the characteristic-zero Adams difference

\[
\Psi^p(\mathcal A)
=
\operatorname{Sym}^p\mathcal A
-
\det(\mathcal A)\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^{p-2}\mathcal A.
\]

Three previously separate observations now have one explanation:

1. **Local inertia collapse.** The Adams difference becomes rank two at infinity.
2. **The Dwork boundary.** Haessig's effective decomposition works for odd `k<p` and explicitly fails to pass automatically to `k>=p` because denominators cease to be p-adic units. The first excluded value is exactly `k=p`, where the modular exact sequence appears.
3. **P-adic divisibility.** Exact computation at every calibrated prime `p=11,17,23,29,41,47,53` gives

   \[
   v_p(T_p)=\frac{p+4}{3},
   \]

   consistent with a special Frobenius/Cartier filtration at this boundary.

This is the first mechanism found that is simultaneously global, cross-symmetric-power and specific to `k=p`.

## 5. Why this is not yet the complex bound

The sequence exists after reduction to coefficient characteristic `p`. The desired estimate is an archimedean bound for a characteristic-zero Frobenius trace.

In characteristic zero, the geometric monodromy is `SL_2` and

\[
[\operatorname{Sym}^p]-[\operatorname{Sym}^{p-2}]
\]

is genuinely virtual; no corresponding rank-two exact sequence exists. Therefore the modular sequence directly gives congruences and p-adic filtrations, but not cancellation of complex Frobenius eigenvalues.

Even a proof of

\[
p^{(p+4)/3}\mid T_p
\]

would leave the desired estimate equivalent to

\[
\left|\frac{T_p}{p^{(p+4)/3}}\right|
\le C p^{(p-11)/6}.
\]

The current Deligne/GOS estimate gives the same right-hand side with an additional factor `p-3`. Thus p-adic divisibility alone does not remove the decisive factor of order `p`.

The promising form of Route 1 is no longer to compare the two symmetric powers abstractly. It is to lift the modular exact sequence to the Dwork cohomology complexes.

### OPEN boundary-Adams lifting theorem

\[
0\to F^*\mathcal A
