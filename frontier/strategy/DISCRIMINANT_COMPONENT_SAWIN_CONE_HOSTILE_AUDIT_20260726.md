# Hostile audit of the discriminant-component and Sawin-cone obstruction

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Verdict:** the obstruction is valid if and only if the identified affine scheme is exactly Sawin's `X_(p,p-4,0)` and the cone/torsor cohomology bookkeeping is correct. Both points survive the checks below.

## 1. Could Sawin's coefficient vector be nonzero?

No. In the complete four-parameter interval, the coefficients of `T^(p-1),...,T^4` are fixed to zero. The coefficient of `T` is not fixed: the centre contribution `-T` is absorbed into the varying linear offset. Hence Sawin's fixed vector of the first `p-4` elementary coefficients is exactly zero.

## 2. Could Newton identities fail in characteristic `p`?

Not in the used range. Only indices `1,...,p-4` are inverted. All are units in characteristic `p`. Thus the elementary-symmetric and power-sum ideals agree.

## 3. Does the nonexistence of an ordinary lift of `W` invalidate the quotient?

No. The quotient is a geometric quotient in characteristic `p` by the actual diagonal additive subgroup. The map is an `S_p`-equivariant `G_a` torsor, and `Rq_! Q_l=Q_l(-1)[-2]` follows fibrewise. No characteristic-zero representation lifting is used.

## 4. Could the cone vertex cancel the primitive hooks?

No. The vertex is fixed and carries only the trivial representation. Localization from the punctured cone to the cone is therefore an isomorphism on every nontrivial isotypic component.

## 5. Could the Euler class of the `G_m` bundle cancel a primitive hook?

The relevant Euler map is cup product with the hyperplane class. It maps a nontrivial `S_p` type in `H^2_prim(Y)` to `H^4(Y)`, which is trivial as an `S_p` representation. Equivariance forces that map to vanish. The primitive multiplicity therefore appears in both adjacent cone degrees.

## 6. Are degrees 5 and 6 included in Sawin's `B(pi)`?

Yes. The affine variety has dimension four. Sawin sums compactly supported cohomology through degree `2 dim X-1=7`. Both degrees are charged.

## 7. Could the residue sign twist change the dimensions?

No. It reverses the hook indexing. The committed primitive profiles already include that reversal. Twisting does not reduce total multiplicity.

## 8. Could the ordinary discriminant have hidden extra components?

For a selected pair collision, translation reduces the normalization to the ordered-root cover of `Z^(p-2)+AZ+B`. The specialization `A=1` has `S_(p-2)` geometric monodromy by the simple-critical-point transposition graph. Therefore the generic ordered cover is connected and the pair-collision divisor is irreducible. All components are the pair orbit.

## 9. Does a resolution of the singular discriminant introduce high-hook exceptional divisors?

It may introduce complicated intermediate representations, but it cannot change the actual cohomology of Sawin's affine cone already computed from the smooth projective base. Nor can it change `H^2` of the reduced boundary curve, which is generated by its irreducible components. Such a resolution cannot rescue the absolute Betti inequality.

## 10. Is the crown refuted?

No. The refuted statement is only that Sawin's absolute error estimate with `B_Lambda<=p-1` can close the crown for this family. The trace may be far smaller than the sum of Betti dimensions because of Frobenius cancellation between the large parity-balanced multiplicity spaces.

## Final ruling

The previous programme status understated the consequence of the compactified hook calculation. The projective primitive page is not merely an upstream approximation to Sawin's Betti complex; through the affine cone and translation torsor, it supplies the actual nontrivial cohomology charged by `B(pi)`. The aggregate unsigned-Betti route is therefore closed. The only live use of the Cayley/Airy machinery is a direct Frobenius-trace comparison.
