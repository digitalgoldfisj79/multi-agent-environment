# Local inertia of the p-th Adams irreducibility defect

**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Status:** exact theorem, conditional only on the already proved local-inertia description of the normalized degree-`p` cover. It shows that the Adams irreducibility defect vanishes on both finite inertia groups and has one explicit virtual representation at wild infinity.

## 1. Adams defect character

Let `P` be the natural rank-`p` permutation representation of `S_p` and put

`W=psi^p(P)-P`

in the rational representation ring.

For `sigma in S_p`,

`chi_W(sigma)=Tr(sigma^p|P)-Tr(sigma|P).`

Since `p` is prime, a cycle of length at most `p` contributes new fixed points under the p-th power only if it has length `p`. Hence

`boxed( chi_W(sigma)=p if sigma is a p-cycle, and 0 otherwise. )`

In particular `W` has virtual rank zero.

## 2. Finite branch divisors

For the root-negation descended map

`R_a(c,Y)=Y(Y^((p-1)/2)+aY+c)^2`,

the generic inertia along `e=B_a(c)` is generated by one transposition.

Along `e=0`, the roots of `H_c(Y)=Y^m+aY+c` are the simultaneous simple critical points of `R_a`; generic inertia is generated by the product of `m=(p-1)/2` disjoint transpositions, with the remaining sheet fixed.

Both finite inertia images have order two. Neither their identity nor their nontrivial element is a p-cycle. Therefore:

### Theorem ADLI.1 — finite-inertia annihilation

`boxed( W|_(I_(e=0))=0,   W|_(I_(e=B))=0 )`

in the rational representation ring.

The same remains true after tensoring by the square-value Kummer projector, because zero remains zero.

Thus the generic finite branch divisors contribute no Adams-defect local system. Any finite-boundary contribution can occur only at the collision locus of the branch strata, not along their generic points.

## 3. Wild infinity

The proved geometric inertia group at infinity is

`I_infinity=C_p semidirect C_m`, `m=(p-1)/2`,

acting affinely on `F_p`; the tame multiplier group consists of the squares.

The p-cycles in this affine group are exactly the nonidentity translations. Hence the restricted character of `W` is

- `0` at the identity;
- `p` at every nonidentity translation;
- `0` at every element with nontrivial multiplier.

Let `V=P-1` be the standard representation restricted to `I_infinity`, and let

`Q=Reg_(I_infinity/C_p)`

be the regular representation of the tame quotient, inflated to `I_infinity`. Its character is `m` on every element of `C_p` and zero outside `C_p`. Direct character comparison gives:

### Theorem ADLI.2 — explicit infinity restriction

`boxed( W|_(I_infinity)=-V+2Q. )`

Indeed:

- at the identity: `-(p-1)+2m=0`;
- at a nontrivial translation: `-(-1)+2m=p`;
- outside `C_p`: both terms have trace zero.

## 4. Consequence for localization

The Adams defect is therefore locally trivial on the generic finite discriminant and is controlled at infinity by only:

- one copy of the standard wild representation, with negative virtual sign;
- two copies of a purely tame quotient regular representation.

This is a substantially smaller local object than the full alternating hook ledger. A cyclic-Adams localization proof of the weight-three lemma needs to analyse only:

1. wild infinity with the displayed `-V+2Q` class;
2. the finite set/scheme where `e=0` and `e=B_a(c)` collide;
3. the corners introduced by compactification.

It need not estimate the generic finite branch divisors separately.

## 5. Limit

The collision scheme of the two finite branch components still has degree growing with `p` over the algebraic closure. ADLI.1 does not by itself prove that its localized contribution has bounded Betti number; a second cancellation or a uniform correspondence is required there.

## 6. Epistemic classification

- Adams character formula: exact representation theory.
- Finite inertia cycle types: exact from the descended derivative and the proved local cover model.
- Finite-inertia annihilation: exact.
- Infinity character and `-V+2Q` identity: exact.
- Uniform control of branch-collision and compactification strata: open.
- Cyclic-Adams Weight-Three Lemma: open.
- Function-field d=1 crown: open.
