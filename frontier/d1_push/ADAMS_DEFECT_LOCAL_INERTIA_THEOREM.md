# Local inertia of the p-th Adams irreducibility defect

**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Status:** exact theorem, conditional only on the already proved local-inertia description of the normalized degree-`p` cover. It shows that the Adams irreducibility defect vanishes on every finite inertia stratum and has one explicit virtual representation at wild infinity.

## 1. Adams defect character

Let `P` be the natural rank-`p` permutation representation of `S_p` and put

`W=psi^p(P)-P`

in the rational representation ring.

For `sigma in S_p`,

`chi_W(sigma)=Tr(sigma^p|P)-Tr(sigma|P).`

Since `p` is prime, a cycle of length at most `p` contributes new fixed points under the p-th power only if it has length `p`. Hence

`boxed( chi_W(sigma)=p if sigma is a p-cycle, and 0 otherwise. )`

In particular `W` has virtual rank zero.

## 2. Generic finite branch divisors

For the root-negation descended map

`R_a(c,Y)=Y(Y^((p-1)/2)+aY+c)^2`,

the generic inertia along `e=B_a(c)` is generated by one transposition.

Along `e=0`, the roots of `H_c(Y)=Y^m+aY+c` are the simultaneous simple critical points of `R_a`; generic inertia is generated by the product of `m=(p-1)/2` disjoint transpositions, with the remaining sheet fixed.

Both finite inertia images have order two. Neither their identity nor their nontrivial element is a p-cycle. Therefore

`W|_(I_(e=0))=0,   W|_(I_(e=B))=0`

in the rational representation ring.

## 3. Finite collision strata

The two finite branch components meet exactly when `B_a(c)=0`.

Write `y_0=-c/(3a)`. Since

`B_a(c)=y_0 H_c(y_0)^2`,

there are two local types.

### 3.1 The point `c=0`

Here `y_0=0` and

`H_0(Y)=Y(Y^(m-1)+a)`,

so the special fibre is

`R_a(0,Y)=Y^3(Y^(m-1)+a)^2`.

Its root clusters have sizes `3` at zero and `2` at every other root.

### 3.2 Nonzero collisions

If `c!=0` and `H_c(y_0)=0`, then

`y_0^(m-1)=2a`.

Moreover

`H'_c(y_0)=m y_0^(m-1)+a=0`,

while `H''_c(y_0)!=0`. Thus `H_c` has an exact double root at `y_0`. The special fibre `R_a(c,Y)` has one root cluster of size `4`, clusters of size `2` at the other roots of `H_c`, and one simple root at zero.

Over the strict henselization at a collision point, the factors attached to distinct root clusters remain coprime. Local inertia therefore preserves each cluster. Every local cycle has length at most `4`, while `p>=5`; no local inertia element is a p-cycle.

### Theorem ADLI.1 — complete finite-inertia annihilation

`boxed( W|_(I_x)=0 for every finite boundary point x. )`

The same remains true after tensoring by the square-value Kummer projector.

Thus the p-th Adams irreducibility defect has no finite local contribution, even though the collision scheme has degree growing with `p` over the algebraic closure.

## 4. Wild infinity

The proved geometric inertia group at infinity is

`I_infinity=C_p semidirect C_m`, `m=(p-1)/2`,

acting affinely on `F_p`; the tame multiplier group consists of the squares.

The p-cycles in this affine group are exactly the nonidentity translations. Hence the restricted character of `W` is

- `0` at the identity;
- `p` at every nonidentity translation;
- `0` at every element with nontrivial multiplier.

Let `V=P-1` be the standard representation restricted to `I_infinity`, and let

`Q=Reg_(I_infinity/C_p)`

be the regular representation of the tame quotient, inflated to `I_infinity`. Its character is `m` on every element of `C_p` and zero outside `C_p`. Direct character comparison gives:

### Theorem ADLI.2 — explicit infinity restriction

`boxed( W|_(I_infinity)=-V+2Q. )`

Indeed:

- at the identity: `-(p-1)+2m=0`;
- at a nontrivial translation: `-(-1)+2m=p`;
- outside `C_p`: both terms have trace zero.

## 5. Consequence for localization

All finite branch and collision strata are annihilated by the Adams character. The localization problem is therefore concentrated at infinity, where the virtual inertia class is exactly

`-V+2Q`.

A proof of the Cyclic-Adams Weight-Three Lemma now needs to analyse only:

1. the wild standard term `-V` at infinity;
2. the two tame quotient-regular terms `2Q`;
3. the corners introduced by a compactification of the infinity divisor.

It does not need a separate estimate for any finite discriminant or collision component.

## 6. Remaining limit

ADLI.2 still contains representations of dimension proportional to `p`. The theorem localizes the entire obstruction but does not yet prove the required cancellation between the wild standard term and the tame quotient terms after global pushforward along the parameter line.

That infinity cancellation is now the unique local theorem required by the Adams route.
