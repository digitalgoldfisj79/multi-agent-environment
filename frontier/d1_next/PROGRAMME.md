## 3. Primary lane: Airy boundary transport

**Pass:** one auditable table in which every term of the source ledger maps to a literal target object or an explicit correction term.

**Fail:** an essential source or target object cannot be defined independently of first-trace numerics.

No remote compute is permitted at this gate.

### Gate ABT-1 — invariant compatibility

- rank and virtual rank;

**Pass:** no invariant mismatch remains after the explicit correction ledger.

## 4. Fallback lane: integral Tate-diagonal lift

The fallback starts only after ABT is either completed or closed by a theorem-level obstruction.

### Gate ITD-0 — integral model and comparison map

Freeze a perfect `C_p`-equivariant integral Airy complex with Frobenius whose generic cyclic trace realizes `R_p` and whose modular Smith/Tate localization realizes the rank-two Frobenius contraction.

The map must be integral and Weil compatible. A comparison only after reduction mod `p` is insufficient.

### Gate ITD-1 — bounded-cone attempt

Test constructions that alter the natural lift without changing the generic trace, including excision, relative compact support, localization at the root-direction boundary and derived norm filtrations.

**Pass:** a comparison cone with uniformly bounded generic rank after the explicit Tate correction.

**Fail:** a theorem proving every admissible construction in the chosen class has growing cone.

Raw rank experiments cannot close this gate.

### Gate ITD-2 — linear-cone cancellation

- purity and exact weight;

- absence or explicit subtraction of trivial constituents;
- absence or explicit subtraction of Artin–Schreier constituents;

  \[
  O(p^{(p+1)/2}).

This is the genuinely new analytic theorem if ABT fails.

### Gate ITD-3 — analytic propagation
