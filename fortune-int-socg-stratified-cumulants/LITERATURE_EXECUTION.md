## Local constants

- Gallagher-type singular-series averaging and Pintz's uniform variants control averages of local Hardy--Littlewood constants over offset sets.
- Kuperberg extends singular-series averaging to larger sets and uses it in conditional prime-count tail problems.

These results support the C4 local-factor model, but they do not estimate prime incidence at the deterministic centres `P_j`.

## Multiplicative products modulo primes

Results on products of intervals or multiplicative subgroups modulo a prime concern independently varying product variables or fixed subgroup orbits. They do not directly control the deterministic partial-product sequence of consecutive terminal primes. C5 therefore proves the available collision large-sieve lemma directly and leaves the weighted composite-modulus extension explicit.
