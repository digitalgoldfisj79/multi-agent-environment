\[
\sum_{\substack{n\in\mathcal M_b\\n\ne m}}

\sum_{p>2X}\frac{N_p(m)}p,

where `N_p(m)` counts candidate primes congruent to `m mod p`. For `2X<p<=H/2`, Brun--Titchmarsh gives

\[
N_p(m)\ll\frac{H}{p\log(H/p)}.
