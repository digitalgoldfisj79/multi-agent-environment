# C8 — conditional bridge and falsification

## Conditional benchmark

State `RUHL-SOCG`, a row-uniform Hardy--Littlewood hypothesis for selected primorial centres, through the growing tuple order required by the stratum temperature, with an aggregate error below the one-stratum detector budget.

Verify exactly whether it implies `INT-SOCG` directly or only the inherited `INT-AOD` Bonferroni bridge.

## Diagnostics

- exact low-order ordinary cumulants on primorial panels;
- equality-pattern contributions;
- local collision-prime statistics;
- additive and multiplicative residue traces of the primorial walk;
- numerical Laplace zero radii within frozen strata;
- adversarial incidence matrices matching all statistics used by a proposed proof.

## Rules

Diagnostics may choose between C4, C5 and C7. They may not set proof parameters or establish asymptotic claims.

A proposed sufficient statistic fails if a registered zero-row surrogate matches it.
