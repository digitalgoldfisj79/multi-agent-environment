# Literature audit

## Friedlander–Iwaniec — Asymptotic sieve for primes

The theorem detects primes in a nonnegative sequence under classical divisor-sum information plus a Möbius-weighted bilinear axiom. In its published form the sequence parameter `x` is the magnitude of the integers whose primality is detected, the remainder level satisfies `D>x^(2/3)`, and the bilinear range is tied to `sqrt(x)`.

**Use here:** exact eligibility audit and identification of the parity-breaking bilinear anatomy.

**Failure here:** `x asymp P_j=exp((1+o(1))X)`, while all ordinary offset distribution is polynomial in `X`.

## Barban–Davenport–Halberstam

The classical mean-square theorem controls the aggregate discrepancy of weighted primes in reduced residue classes:

\[
\sum_{q\le Q}\sum_{(a,q)=1}
|\vartheta(H;q,a)-H/\varphi(q)|^2
\ll HQ\log H
\]

in the range required by M4.

**Use here:** evaluate selected residues `a=-P_j mod q` after Cauchy–Schwarz.

**Additional input required:** the exact multiplicity energy of those selected residues, supplied by the primorial collision theorem from PR #57.

## Bombieri–Vinogradov

The first-moment modulus average alone reaches only the square-root scale of the offset length. Since the outputs are already pre-sieved through primes of size `X=H^(1/2)`, this gives no substantial post-terminal sifting interval.

**Use here:** comparison and scale falsification, not the M5 proof.

## Friedlander–Iwaniec weighted switching and asymptotic-sieve descendants

These methods can break parity when a sequence-specific bilinear or switched-coordinate theorem is available.

**Use here:** formulation of admissible successor mechanisms.

**Not supplied:** a theorem uniform over deterministic primorial microblocks with output factors at exponential scale.

## Governing bridge test

An external theorem is admitted only if:

1. its row averaging is over the deterministic primorial path;
2. its offset window includes `H=X^2/2`;
3. its error is uniform for every registered microblock;
4. all residue multiplicities are included;
5. its final conclusion controls the signed parity tail, not merely divisor incidence or almost primes.
